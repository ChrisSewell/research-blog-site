About

I run a small honeypot at home and take apart what lands in it. Posts here cover the malware it captures, the campaigns behind it, and the indicators I share with MalwareBazaar, URLhaus and ThreatFox.

Before I call anything new, I check it against public reporting, and I credit whoever got there first. All analysis is static, done in an offline sandbox. Indicators are defanged, and no samples are hosted on this site.
