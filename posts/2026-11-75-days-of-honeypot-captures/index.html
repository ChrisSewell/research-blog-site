Worth noting

One /24, three families. 77.239.124[.]0/24 served std000 Mirai (.124), superior (.167) and systemd-baby (.172). superior was first reported by eFeSpain; my notes on its debug build are here.

A Go bot filed as Mirai. Built with -X main.ServerIP and -X main.BotToken. MalwareBazaar calls it Mirai; it’s Go. Its C2, 176.65.139[.]201, shares a /24 with a Mirai campaign spreading through an applyapp.cgi router injection.

Fast rebuilds. jkl Mirai: 5 builds in 5 days. superior’s dropper: 5 revisions in one day. Hash IOCs go stale fast.

Bots evicting bots. superior ships competitor killers; an Outlaw .rsync kit carries a clean.sh for rival miners.

Android boxes. superior persists via ADB on 5555 and Magisk/KernelSU, then squats the port.
